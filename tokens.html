3. Add a Memo (Mandatory)

You must include a memo with the Project ID and your Solana wallet address.

ID: citarum-river | MY_WALLET: YourSolanaAddressHere

4. Receive Your RWA Token

Once funds are received by the multisig, we mint your receipt token. You can track the token's status (🟡 Funding → 🟢 Verified) in your Phantom or Solflare wallet.

Important Rules

  • Not an investment: RWA tokens are proof of impact, not equity.
  • No Memo = No Token: Without a project ID, we cannot issue your receipt.
  • Multisig Security: Funds are held in a 2-of-3 multisig wallet.
